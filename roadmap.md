
# Roadmap

## Current Stage

HoloLedger currently focuses on the core workflow required by a Pokémon TCG seller:

- Authentication
- Profitability calculation
- Fee handling
- Operation tracking
- Persistent data
- Dashboard overview

The next phases would expand the application from an operation tracker into a broader seller-management platform.

---

## Short-Term Improvements

### Improved Dashboard

Potential improvements:

- More profitability metrics
- Marketplace comparisons
- Time-based performance views
- Improved filtering
- Better operation summaries

---

### Reporting

Planned reporting functionality may include:

- Monthly profit reports
- Marketplace performance
- Expense summaries
- CSV export
- PDF reports

---

### Inventory Management

Future versions could allow users to track:

- Cards owned
- Acquisition price
- Quantity
- Current status
- Listing marketplace
- Sale history

---

## Medium-Term Goals

### Marketplace Integrations

Potential integrations include:

- Cardmarket
- eBay
- Other European marketplaces

Possible functionality:

- Importing operations
- Synchronizing listings
- Retrieving marketplace fees
- Reducing manual data entry

---

### Price Tracking

A future price-tracking system could provide:

- Historical card prices
- Acquisition vs current value
- Unrealized profit
- Market trends

---

### Advanced Analytics

Potential analytics include:

- Profit by marketplace
- Average margin
- Best-performing products
- Monthly trends
- Expense distribution
- Capital turnover

---

## Security Improvements

Future security work may include:

- Audit logging
- Rate limiting
- Stronger input validation
- Security headers
- Dependency scanning
- Automated SAST / SCA
- Improved monitoring
- Session security improvements

---

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to evolve HoloLedger into a lightweight management platform for collectible-card sellers.

Possible areas include:

- Inventory
- Sales
- Expenses
- Analytics
- Marketplace integrations
- Pricing
- Reporting

The project will continue to prioritize simple workflows, maintainable architecture and secure handling of user data.
